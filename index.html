How to read this page

Each tag grades the sentence it sits under, not the picture. Where we hold a screenshot and found no such control, the tag reads “not on this screen”. “None found” means we hold no picture and found nothing in public sources. Neither tag is proof that the product lacks the feature.

seen in useWe saw it in the running product, a screenshot, a public API or the shipped JavaScript.
in vendor docsThe vendor states it in their own help centre, policy or guide.
vendor saysA marketing assertion with no independent support.
none foundWe found no such thing in public sources. This is a gap in our knowledge, not proof of absence.
not applicableThe stage is not a question this product answers. We did not search, thus this is not a gap.
